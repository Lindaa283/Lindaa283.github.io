    Changelog

    What changed, not what was promised.

    A public record of shipped work, changed beliefs and honest corrections. The newest entry is the current version of this living CV.

    1. 28 Nov 2026

      Open Source Life v0.1

      Reframed this site as a living CV: evidence of work, current open loops, a public collaboration route and explicit privacy boundaries. Added a structured GitHub Issue for proposals.

    2. 22 Nov 2026

      The week a model wrote seventeen million lines of proof

      A mathematician spent twelve years on 940 pages. Eight hours after it went public, somebody found a hole in the draft using a model. I keep thinking about the line he wrote in the paper — we are still here, we are not leaving. I am 21 and I have no idea which half of that story I am in.

    3. 10 Nov 2026

      Two labs, two grading systems

      One programme taught me never to leave a number unlogged. The other taught me to say the limitation out loud before anyone asks. The first makes the work correct; the second makes people believe it. I needed both, and neither mentions the other.

    4. 28 Oct 2026

      A support ticket is a price signal

      Most messages to the shop are not about the product. They are about the same four minutes of confusion. I wrote those four minutes up as a guide, and the refund rate halved. The lesson was embarrassing and cheap: read your own tickets before you build anything new.

    5. 14 Oct 2026

      Run forty-one

      The phase separation would not behave. Forty runs, no pattern. My supervisor suggested shelving it. I logged every one anyway, and the null result is the part of my notebook I would actually show somebody.

    6. 30 Sep 2026

      Reading a filing in a second language

      I am being trained to work inside the pharmaceutical industry. Nobody on the course has ever shown us who owns it. So I started reading annual reports — slowly, with a dictionary — from the other side of the table.
