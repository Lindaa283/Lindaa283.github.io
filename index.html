The market

I’m 21.
My only real edge is time.

US equities are where I learned that patience is a skill, not a personality trait. I am not here for a ten-bagger this quarter. I am here because a 21-year-old who keeps adding and does not panic beats a 40-year-old who starts late and trades well.

So I read filings in a second language, I watch the pharma industry I am being trained to work in from the owner’s side of the table, and I keep the position boring on purpose.

Illustration of compounding mathematics, not my portfolio, not advice, and not a claim about any return I have made.

Notes

Working in the open.

Everything I learn, build and get wrong, written down as it happens. Shorter and rougher than a blog, because the point is the frequency, not the polish.

  1. 22 Nov 2026

    The week a model wrote seventeen million lines of proof

    A mathematician spent twelve years on 940 pages. Eight hours after it went public, somebody found a hole in the draft using a model. I keep thinking about the line he wrote in the paper — we are still here, we are not leaving. I am 21 and I have no idea which half of that story I am in.

  2. 10 Nov 2026

    Two labs, two grading systems, one visa

    Nanjing taught me to never leave a number unlogged. Dublin taught me to say the limitation out loud before anyone asks. The first makes the work correct; the second makes people believe it. I needed both and neither programme mentions the other.

  3. 28 Oct 2026

    A support ticket is a price signal

    Most messages to the shop are not about the product. They are about the same four minutes of confusion. I wrote those four minutes up as a guide, and the refund rate halved. The lesson was embarrassing and cheap: read your own tickets before you build anything new.

  4. 14 Oct 2026

    Run forty-one

    The phase separation would not behave. Forty runs, no pattern. My supervisor suggested shelving it. I logged every one anyway, and the null result is the part of my notebook I would actually show somebody.

  5. 30 Sep 2026

    Reading a filing in a second language

    I am being trained to work inside the pharmaceutical industry. Nobody on the course has ever shown us who owns it. So I started reading annual reports — slowly, with a dictionary — from the other side of the table.
