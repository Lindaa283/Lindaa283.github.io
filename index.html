The market

I’m 21.
My only real edge is time.

US equities are where I learned that patience is a skill, not a personality trait. I am not here for a ten-bagger this quarter. I am here because a 21-year-old who keeps adding and does not panic beats a 40-year-old who starts late and trades well.

So I read filings in a second language, I watch the pharma industry I am being trained to work in from the owner’s side of the table, and I keep the position boring on purpose.

Illustration of compounding mathematics, not my portfolio, not advice, and not a claim about any return I have made.

Manifesto

Curious about who writes the rules of the game.

On class. I don’t think the interesting question is who has money. It’s who gets to set the terms — and how much of that is inherited story rather than fate. I’d rather learn those rules in public than resent them in private.

On being read. Girl, 21, pink, cosplay. That read is not an insult and I don’t need it corrected; I need it priced. Underestimation is a discount, and I am buying.

On adventure. I left Nanjing for Dublin, opened a shop, started posting my whole learning process online as an introvert, and I’m pointing all of it at building a company worth backing.
